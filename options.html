About

Cookie Profile Switcher allows you to manage multiple cookie sessions for your websites. No more logging out and logging in just to switch accounts. Create a new profile, sign in once, then switch between profiles as needed.


How it Works

The extension uses the browser cookie and storage APIs. When a new profile is created, the profile name is stored in local storage. When you switch profiles, cookies from the active site are saved to the previous profile and cookies from the selected profile are loaded into the browser.
